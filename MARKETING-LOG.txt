2. TARGET PERSONAS & VALUE PROPOSITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persona 1: German-Speaking Freelancers & Small Business Owners
  * Pain: Monthly manual searching through crowded mailboxes for PDF invoices,
    receipts, and subscription statements, followed by tedious manual filing.
  * Solution: Automated profile-based retrieval (IMAP / Gmail), automatic attachment
    conversion to standardized PDF, private local archive, and DATEV export.
- Persona 2: Tax Advisors, Accountants & Bookkeepers
  * Pain: Inconsistent receipt formats, missing document numbers, and broken date
    or decimal formatting causing imports into DATEV Unternehmen online to fail.
  * Solution: Built-in DATEV cp1252 EXTF CSV export compliant with Buchungsstapel
    guidelines, with pre-save account validation (4-8 digits) and robust amount parsing.
- Persona 3: Privacy-Conscious Individuals & GDPR Officers
  * Pain: Cloud invoice aggregation tools require full read access to mailboxes and
    store financial documents on external third-party servers.
  * Solution: 100% Local-First Execution (INV-LOCAL-01). Credentials stay encrypted in
    Windows Credential Vault (DPAPI), documents reside exclusively on the user's SSD.
- Persona 4: Python Desktop & Open-Source Developers
  * Pain: Fragile GUI wrappers with tight coupling, untestable dialog popups, and
    poorly structured test suites.
  * Solution: Clean PySide6 architecture, decoupled headless CSV export routines,
    203+ automated tests, and rich documentation under the permissive MIT license.
