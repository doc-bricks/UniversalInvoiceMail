### Description (max 10,000 chars)
UniversalInvoiceMail is a local-first desktop application designed for freelancers, businesses, and organizations that need to automatically collect incoming invoices from email accounts, convert receipts to PDF, and export accounting data without relying on external cloud services.

The application connects to standard IMAP mailboxes or Gmail accounts, discovers invoice emails, downloads attachments, converts image and office formats locally to standardized PDFs, and prepares bookings for DATEV export.

**Key Features:**

- **Automated Invoice Collection:** Fetch invoices via encrypted IMAP (SSL/TLS) or Google Mail APIs directly from your accounts.
- **Multi-Format PDF Conversion:** Converts pictures (PNG, JPG, TIFF), spreadsheets (XLSX), office documents (DOCX), and email containers (EML, MSG) locally to PDF.
- **Local OCR Engine:** Offline text recognition and invoice field detection for scanned receipts using Tesseract and pypdfium2.
- **DATEV CSV Export:** Generate standard DATEV booking batches (CSV cp1252) with SKR03 and SKR04 chart-of-accounts mapping for your accountant.
- **Windows Credential Manager Integration:** Account passwords remain secured inside the Windows Credential Manager rather than plain text configuration files.
- **PWA Mobile Companion:** Export privacy-redacted bundles (no passwords, tokens, or email bodies) for mobile review on phones or tablets.
- **Audit-Ready Storage:** Structured local folders for all original receipts and converted PDF documents.

**Why UniversalInvoiceMail?**

- **Privacy-First:** Sensitive financial documents and emails stay strictly on your local PC.
- **No Cloud Lock-In:** No subscription fees, no telemetry, and no third-party cloud servers.
- **Accelerate Accounting:** Eliminate manual receipt searching and save hours each month.
