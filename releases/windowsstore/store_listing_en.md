Short Description:
Local invoice hub: fetch emails, archive receipts as PDF and export DATEV-compatible booking batches.

Description:
UniversalInvoiceMail is a local-first desktop application designed for freelancers, businesses, and organizations that need to automatically collect incoming invoices from email accounts, convert receipts to PDF, and export accounting data without relying on external cloud services.

The application connects to standard IMAP mailboxes or Google Mail accounts, discovers invoice emails, downloads attachments, converts image and office formats locally to standardized PDFs, and prepares bookings for DATEV export.

- Automated invoice collection via encrypted IMAP (SSL/TLS) or Google Mail
- Multi-format local PDF conversion for images (PNG, JPG, BMP, TIFF) and documents (DOCX, XLSX, EML, MSG)
- Local text recognition and invoice field detection without sending files to third-party servers
- DATEV-compliant booking batch export (CSV cp1252) supporting SKR03 and SKR04 chart-of-accounts
- Windows Credential Manager integration for secure password storage
- PWA Mobile Companion for privacy-redacted mobile review without cloud dependencies
- Audit-ready structured storage for all original receipts and converted PDF documents

Target Audience:
Freelancers, self-employed professionals, bookkeepers, and small businesses seeking an automated, privacy-compliant workflow to prepare invoice receipts for accountants and tax advisors.
